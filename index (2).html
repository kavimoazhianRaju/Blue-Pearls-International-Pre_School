About Us

Learn about the heart and people behind Blue Pearls International Pre School In Chennai.

Our School

Blue Pearls International Pre School In Chennai is a play-based early learning centre for children aged 1.5 to 6 years. Our bright, child-friendly campus blends modern early-childhood methods with warm, personal attention so every child feels confident and curious on the first day and every day after.


  • Play-based, activity-driven curriculum
  • Trained and caring teachers
  • Safe, hygienic, child-friendly campus
  • Strong parent–school partnership
🏫

Vision & Mission

👁️

Our Vision

To be a place where every child discovers the joy of learning, grows into a kind and confident individual, and builds a strong foundation for lifelong success.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide a secure, stimulating and loving environment that nurtures social, emotional, physical and intellectual development through creative, play-based learning.

Our Founder

Mrs. Shymala — Founder & Director


With over two decades of experience in early childhood education, Mrs. Shymala founded Blue Pearls International Pre School In Chennai with one belief: every child is unique and deserves a joyful start to learning. Her vision of a school that feels like a second home continues to guide everything we do.


“Children don’t learn to bloom — they are born to. Our job is simply to give them sunshine.”

Our Programs

Age-appropriate learning that grows with your child.

🧸1.5 – 2.5 yrs

Playgroup

A gentle first step into school. Sensory play, rhymes, songs and free movement help toddlers adjust, socialise and explore.

🎨2.5 – 3.5 yrs

Nursery

Builds language, motor skills and curiosity through stories, colours, shapes, art and guided play.

🔤3.5 – 4.5 yrs

LKG

Introduces phonics, early reading, number sense and good habits with hands-on, joyful activities.

📚4.5 – 5.5 yrs

UKG

Prepares children for Grade 1 with reading, writing, basic maths, science exploration and confidence building.

Parent Guidelines

  • Please drop and pick up your child on time.
  • Send your child in the prescribed uniform with a labelled bag and water bottle.
  • Only authorised persons listed in school records may collect the child.
  • Keep your child at home if unwell and inform the class teacher.
  • Send healthy, nut-free snacks wherever possible.
  • Attend Parent–Teacher meetings and read circulars regularly.

FAQs

What is the teacher–child ratio?

We maintain about 1 teacher for every 10 children, with an additional helper in Playgroup.

How do you help children settle in?

We offer a gradual orientation, shorter initial days and a warm, patient approach so children feel secure.

Is my child required to be toilet-trained?

Not for Playgroup. Our caregivers gently support toilet learning.

Do you provide transport and day care?

Yes, GPS-enabled school buses and an optional day care are available.

How can I schedule a campus visit?

Call us, use the contact form below, or simply walk in during school hours.
